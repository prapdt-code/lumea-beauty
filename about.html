Behind Luméa Beauty

We believe makeup and skincare should celebrate natural nuance, texture, and individual self-expression.

Origin

Founded on Intention, Not Transient Trends.

We’re a growing beauty company bringing together creators, marketers, product thinkers, and beauty enthusiasts. Started in 2024 as an indie formulation atelier in Bengaluru, Luméa was born from a simple observation: modern beauty routines felt rushed, prescriptive, and disconnected from real skin.

Instead of producing endless synthetic SKUs, our team spent months experimenting with cold-pressed botanical extracts, skin-barrier supporting lipopeptides, and adaptive mineral pigments that honor every undertone.
